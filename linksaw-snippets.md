## Linksaw Snippets


># Type less. Do more.

Linksaw Snippets is a global text autocomplete and expansion extension for Chrome. Create snippets for your most-used phrases and expand them instantly anywhere you type.

[Add to Chrome — It's Free](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/linksaw-shortcuts-v2/jpnabakldholjnaeffpefggocfpndpei)

---

### ⚡️ Lightning Fast
Just type your trigger key and your predefined phrase expands instantly inside most text fields in your browser.

### ☁️ Cloud Sync
Sync your shortcuts securely across all your devices using Google Sign-In authentication.

### ⚙️ Customizable
Define your own trigger keys, create snippets with or without titles, and effortlessly manage your library.
